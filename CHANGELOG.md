# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-13

- Hold Classic Bestiary's details key over a creature in EasyFind's search results (Ctrl unless you chose another in Classic Bestiary's options): each ability's own text shows under it, with the spells it sets off. The tooltip says which key to hold.
- Needs EasyFind 5.3.0 or newer, which carries the creature rows the abilities sit on.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

- Hover a creature in EasyFind's search results: its tooltip lists the abilities Classic Bestiary knows for it, each with its icon, eight at a glance and a count of the rest.
- A creature Classic Bestiary has nothing for keeps its usual tooltip.
- The Classic Bestiary page of EasyFind's Extensions tab, also opened by its tile: switch the lines off, or choose how many a tooltip lists (4, 8, 12 or all).
- Classic Era and WoW Forever. Needs EasyFind 4.2.0 or newer.
