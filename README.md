# EasyFind: Classic Bestiary Extension

See what a creature casts before you fight it. Hover a creature in EasyFind's search results and its tooltip lists the abilities Classic Bestiary knows for it.

## Requirements

- EasyFind
- Classic Bestiary

Classic Era and WoW Forever. Needs EasyFind 4.2.0 or newer.

## What it does

- Hover a creature's row: under where it stands, its abilities from Classic Bestiary, each with its icon, eight at a glance and a count of the rest.
- A creature Classic Bestiary has nothing for keeps its usual tooltip.
- The Classic Bestiary page of EasyFind's Extensions tab, also opened by its tile: switch the lines off, or choose how many a tooltip lists (4, 8, 12 or all).

Nothing of Classic Bestiary's is copied into this addon: it asks Classic Bestiary for a creature's abilities when you hover its row.
